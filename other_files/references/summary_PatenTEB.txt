### **Executive Overview**
Patent text embeddings are crucial for automated prior art search, technological landscape mapping, and patent analytics. However, general-purpose embedding benchmarks (such as MTEB) lack patent domain coverage, while existing patent datasets focus on narrow, single-task applications. 

To bridge this gap, the paper introduces:
1. **PatenTEB**: A comprehensive evaluation benchmark comprising **15 tasks across 4 task families** with **2.06 million annotated instances**, featuring strict leakage prevention and domain-stratified splits across 109 International Patent Classification (IPC3) categories.
2. **`patembed`**: A specialized, multi-task patent embedding model family spanning **67M to 344M parameters** with context lengths up to **4,096 tokens**.

---

### **1. The PatenTEB Benchmark Design**
PatenTEB addresses key real-world patent matching challenges, such as asymmetric fragment queries and cross-domain semantic matching. All data points are built on **simple patent families** (the DAPFAM strategy) to aggregate equivalent inventions across global jurisdictions.

The benchmark spans **15 tasks** grouped into four core families:
*   **Symmetric Retrieval (3 tasks):** Full-document matching categorized by technological domain relationships—`retrieval_IN` (same IPC3 code), `retrieval_OUT` (disjoint IPC3 codes), and `retrieval_MIXED` (partial IPC3 overlap).
*   **Asymmetric Retrieval (5 tasks):** Fragment-to-full matching scenarios using partial disclosures as queries (`title2full`, `problem2full`, `effect2full`, `problem2solution`, `effect2substance`). Crucially, deterministic fragment removal is applied to target texts to prevent trivial lexical matching.
*   **Classification (3 tasks):** Evaluates multi-class domain taxonomy (`class_text2ipc3` across 109 categories), temporal citation impact trajectory (`class_bloom`), and citation directionality (`class_nli_oldnew`).
*   **Paraphrase Detection (2 tasks):** Evaluates semantic equivalence of technical problem (`para_problem`) and solution (`para_solution`) statements.
*   **Clustering (2 test-only tasks):** Groups patent families by complete IPC codes (`clusters_ext_full_ipc`) and inventor identifiers (`clusters_inventor`).

---

### **2. The `patembed` Model Family & Architecture**
The authors develop the `patembed` model family to support diverse patent workflows without requiring task-specific fine-tuning:

*   **Flagship Model (`patembed-large`):** 344M parameters (1024-dimensional embeddings), initialized from `BERT-for-Patents`.
*   **Distilled Variants:** Compressed via layer subsampling and Knowledge Distillation into smaller students: `patembed-base` (193M / 768D), `patembed-base_small` (143M / 512D), `patembed-small` (117M / 384D), `patembed-mini` (92M / 256D), and `patembed-nano` (67M / 128D).
*   **Long-Context Variants:** Models with extended context windows (`patembed-base_long_1024`, `_2048`, `_4096`) initialized from `gte-modernbert-base` to handle longer documents.
*   **Multi-Task Training:** Jointly trained on 13 tasks using uniform loss weighting. The framework combines:
    *   *Multiple Negatives Ranking Loss (InfoNCE)* for retrieval.
    *   *Online Contrastive Loss* for paraphrase detection.
    *   *Batch-Hard Soft-Margin Triplet Loss* for single-document classification.
    *   *Cross-Entropy Loss* for pairwise classification.
*   **Task Conditioning:** Employs task-specific prepended prompts (e.g., `"encode problem query for solution retrieval:"`) to guide representation learning.

### **3. Key Findings & Empirical Results**

#### **Benchmark & External State-of-the-Art (SOTA)**
*   **Internal Leaderboard:** `patembed-large` achieves the highest overall PatenTEB score (**0.654**), leading in 12 out of 15 tasks. It outperforms general encoders like `Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` (0.558) and specialized baselines like `PAECTER` (0.556).
*   **MTEB SOTA:** On `MTEB BigPatentClustering.v2`, `patembed-base` sets a new SOTA with a **0.494 V-measure**, outperforming `SFR-Embedding-2_R` (0.445) despite being **36× smaller** (193M vs. 7B parameters).
*   **DAPFAM SOTA:** On the cross-domain `DAPFAM` benchmark, `patembed-large` achieves the top score of **0.377 NDCG@100**.

#### **Domain Specialization vs. Parameter Scale**
Domain-pretrained initialization yields massive advantages that raw model scale cannot match. `patembed-base` (193M parameters, patent-pretrained) significantly outperforms `Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` (600M parameters, general text) across patent tasks.

#### **The Cross-Domain Retrieval Challenge**
Retrieval performance degrades **3× to 6×** when matching documents across disjoint technological domains (`retrieval_IN` score of 0.512 vs. `retrieval_OUT` score of 0.172). This highlights that technical vocabulary mismatch across IPC domains remains a fundamental barrier for pure vector embedding models.

---

### **4. Ablation Studies & Practical Deployment Optimizations**

1.  **Multi-Task Trade-Off:** Multi-task training incurs a minor penalty on internal benchmark scores (−0.004) but drastically improves external generalization (+0.062 V-measure on BigPatent).
2.  **Embedding Truncation:** Truncating `patembed-large` embeddings from 1024D down to **256D** reduces vector storage by **4×** while retaining **96.5%** of original performance.
3.  **Distillation Data Efficiency:** Training student models on **54.6%** of the distillation corpus retains **98.3%** of full performance, showing clear diminishing returns beyond half the data.
4.  **Structural Robustness:** The model is highly resilient to missing document parts; removing either abstract or claims results in less than a **1.3%** drop in performance. However, title-only inputs drop performance by **11.6%**, proving that substantive disclosures are required for semantic fidelity.
