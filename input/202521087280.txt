The invention introduces a robust and indigenous design that combines immersion
cooling technology with thermoelectric active cooling to ensure safe, efficient,
15 and uniform thermal management across all battery cells. By enabling simple

[0007] At the core of the invention is a casing that defines an internal
chamber configured to house a plurality of cylindrical battery cells. The cells are
20 fully immersed in a dielectric cooling fluid, which is thermally conductive but
electrically non-conductive, thereby allowing direct liquid contact without risk of
short-circuiting. This immersion arrangement ensures uniform heat transfer from
every surface of the cells, eliminating localized hotspots that are a major cause of
premature cell failure and thermal instability. A specially designed support
25 arrangement is provided at both the top and bottom of the casing to secure the
cells in place, ensuring structural integrity even when the battery pack is subjected
to vibrations, shocks, or non-upright orientations typically encountered in
automotive use.
[0008] To augment passive immersion cooling, the invention integrates a
30 thermoelectric Peltier module mounted at the top of the casing. The Peltier
module is oriented such that its cold side faces the internal chamber, while its hot
4
side faces outward. A thermal transfer plate is coupled to the cold side and
extends into the dielectric fluid so that at least a portion of the plate is submerged,
enabling efficient active cooling of both the fluid and the immersed battery cells.
The hot side of the Peltier module is thermally coupled to an external heat sink,
5 which dissipates the extracted heat into the environment. Through this active
cooling arrangement, the system dynamically lowers the internal temperature of
the battery pack, providing an additional safeguard against thermal runaway
during high load or fast charging conditions.
[0009] The inventive concept lies in the synergistic combination of
10 immersion cooling with dielectric fluid and active thermoelectric heat extraction
through a submerged thermal plate. Unlike conventional air cooling, which
exhibits poor heat transfer, or channel-based liquid cooling, which requires
complex fluid routing and introduces leakage risks, the present invention achieves
direct, uniform, and reliable thermal control across all cells in a compact and
15 modular design. The use of dielectric fluid ensures electrical safety while
providing high thermal conductivity, while the Peltier-based transfer system
provides an additional layer of precision cooling without introducing moving parts
or complex circulation pumps.
[0010] This inventive approach delivers significant technical effects.
20 Experimental studies have demonstrated that while conventional forced air
cooling allows cell temperatures to rise as high as 119°C, the immersion cooling
system of the present invention reduces peak temperatures to approximately 52°C
under similar operating conditions, representing a reduction of nearly 67°C. This
drastic reduction directly mitigates the likelihood of thermal runaway events,
25 thereby enhancing both user safety and battery longevity. Moreover, by
maintaining cells within a safe thermal range, the system improves chargedischarge efficiency, reduces degradation rates, and enables higher power output
for the same battery size.
